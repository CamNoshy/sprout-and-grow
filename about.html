About Sprout & Grow

Organic plants, trusted guidance, and a stronger gardening community in Raleigh.

Our Story

Sprout & Grow was created to bring together quality organic plants, sustainable practices, and accessible gardening education. We believe gardening should feel inspiring rather than overwhelming, whether someone is growing herbs on an apartment balcony or planning a larger backyard garden.

Our nursery is built around transparency, integrity, and environmental stewardship. We want every customer to leave with both healthy plants and the knowledge they need to care for them well.

Our Mission

Educate

Provide resources, workshops, and plant care guidance that help gardeners build confidence.

Sustain

Encourage eco-conscious gardening choices that support healthier homes, gardens, and communities.

Connect

Create a welcoming place where customers can learn, share, and grow together.

The Faces Behind Sprout & Grow

Our team is made up of plant lovers, greenhouse growers, and community-minded educators who care deeply about helping customers succeed. We pride ourselves on being approachable, knowledgeable, and ready to help with plant selection, seasonal advice, and practical solutions for small or large gardening spaces.

What Makes Us Different

  • High-quality organic plants selected for healthy growth
  • Support for home gardeners, urban gardeners, and community groups
  • Seasonal workshops and educational events
  • Friendly guidance that makes gardening more approachable
