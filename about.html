About Sprout & Grow

A welcoming garden center rooted in sustainability, education, and community.

Who We Are

Sprout & Grow was created to make gardening feel easier, more enjoyable, and more approachable for the Raleigh community.

Our greenhouse offers seasonal flowers, houseplants, hanging baskets, succulents, and helpful supplies for gardeners of all levels.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide healthy plants, sustainable gardening resources, and hands-on education that helps people feel confident growing at home.

What We Value

Sustainability

We support gardening choices that are better for people, plants, and the environment.

Education

We believe customers should leave with both beautiful plants and useful knowledge.

Community

We create events and classes that bring local gardeners together.
