Welcome to Sprout & Grow

Sprout & Grow is a greenhouse nursery in Raleigh, North Carolina, focused on offering carefully nurtured organic plants and sustainable gardening solutions. Our goal is to create a welcoming place where home gardeners, eco-conscious shoppers, and community groups can find inspiration, support, and beautiful plant selections.

We believe gardening should be approachable, informative, and rewarding. That is why we combine quality products with helpful resources, seasonal events, and educational opportunities that help our customers grow successfully.

Learn more about regional gardening practices through the NC State Extension Gardener.
